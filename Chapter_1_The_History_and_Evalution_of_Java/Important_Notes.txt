   Bytecode is highly optimised but runs on no real hardware.

             ▼
        Hello.class   ←  bytecode: same file, every platform
             │
     ┌───────┼────────┐
     ▼       ▼        ▼
  JVM on   JVM on   JVM on
  Windows   Mac     Linux
     │       │        │
     ▼       ▼        ▼
   runs    runs     runs
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
